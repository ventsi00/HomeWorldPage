Account & Data Deletion

Request permanent deletion of your account and associated data for HomeWorld.

Option 1: Submit a Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, please contact us to request manual deletion:

  • Send an email to: venstoin@gmail.com
  • Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  • Include your registered email address or username in the body of the email.

What happens when you delete your account?

Upon receiving your request, we will permanently remove all personal information associated with your account, including your profile data and user activity, within 30 days. Some anonymized data may be retained temporarily to comply with legal obligations.
